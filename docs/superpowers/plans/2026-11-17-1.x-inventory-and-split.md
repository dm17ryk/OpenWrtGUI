## 1. What the bench can prove

The bench has three D-Link DWR-921 C3 routers (mt7620, swconfig switch, rt2800 radio: 2.4 GHz only, channels 1-13,
HT20/40) and the BM806C LTE modem on AP2. AP2 is the only router the app may touch. There is no DSA router and no
5 or 6 GHz radio. This splits 1.x into items that can be built and proven now, and items that wait for hardware.

### 3.3 The 0.8.2 image library ledger

Of 43 deferred items, 6 are fixed and 37 open: 6 a person can see, 6 robustness, 25 test, code quality or docs.

A person can see:
- an entry with an empty revision is listed, then refused at download;
- an empty title gives a blank line in Settings and "Image library: , ..." in Discovery;
- the cache age uses the wall clock, so a clock set back keeps a failed read cached;
- the notes and buttons have no `role`/`aria-live`;
- the origin link does not name its release;
- "Import image..." does not look disabled during a download.

Robustness:
- `browser_download_url` is not pinned to https;
- a deep-nesting `RecursionError` on the releases list escapes as a 500;
- the asset size is not compared with `profiles.json`;
- a `profiles.json` without a URL is not counted as skipped;
- the 64 MB hash and copy run on the event loop;
- the `profiles.json` files are fetched one by one under the lock, up to 20 s each (M).

Recommended order 1.1a, 1.1b, 1.2, 1.3, for these reasons:
- 1.1 clears what the reviews found (about 40 open items in 1.1a, none needing hardware), adds the two small
  features that are ready, and needs one short AP2 window.
- Tool pages are the headline phase-2 item, but they need several AP2 windows and their own decisions (iperf vs the
  harness lock, radio writes), so they get a release of their own.
- LTE phase 2 has the most unknowns (no recording, a SIM that can lock), so it goes last of the three.
