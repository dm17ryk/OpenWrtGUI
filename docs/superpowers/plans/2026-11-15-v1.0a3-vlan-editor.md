# v1.0a3 VLAN editor (DSA bridge VLANs) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** LuCI's **Network › Switch/VLAN config** gets a native editor on the Ports screen: the `bridge-vlan`
sections of a DSA router's VLAN-filtering bridge, applied with the existing 60 s rollback. A new simulated DSA
router, `ap5`, proves it, including a change that cuts the app off and comes back by itself.

**Architecture:** One pure module reads the bridge as LuCI does (`ports/bridge.py`), one holds the rules and the
uci ops (`ports/bridge_rules.py`), one reads the state from the router (`ports/bridge_state.py`), and one service
applies it (`ports/bridge_service.py`) through `ApplyRunner.run_planned` with `net_plan(config="network",
rollback=True)`. The verify reads netifd's own report of the bridge's VLANs (`ubus call network.device status`,
already a read). The Ports screen shows a "VLANs · <bridge>" card when the router has no swconfig switch but has a
VLAN-filtering bridge. `ap5` ("AP5 · DSA image") has a DSA board.json, a bridge `switch` with VLAN 1 (lan) and
VLAN 10 (iot), the PC on `lan3`, and a reachability model that follows the bridge VLANs.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13, FastAPI, pydantic 2, pytest (asyncio auto), ruff (line 110), mypy strict, React 19,
TanStack Query, vitest, Playwright e2e on the simulator.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-openwrt-gui-design.md`:
- §5 "Future routers" (the DSA switch backend), §6 Safety (Rollback, Own path), §7.5 Ports, VLANs, mirroring;
- §8, the LuCI parity table: "Network › Switch, Switch/VLAN config → Ports, VLANs, mirroring";
- ROADMAP v1.0: "Native screens for every LuCI page on the bench image (the fallback stays for pages that later
  images bring)".

LuCI facts used here (LuCI master, fetched 2026-11-15; the bench image's LuCI is 26.215.47314~8b5f528):
- **The menu entry** (recorded on AP2, `tests/fixtures/ap2/commands.json`): `admin/network/switch-vlan`, "Switch/VLAN
  config", order 12, view `network/switch-vlan`, `depends.acl` `luci-mod-network-config`. Its `depends.uci` has
  four alternatives: a `device` with `type=bridge, name=switch, vlan_filtering=1`; a `device type=bridge,
  name=switch` with a `bridge-vlan device=switch`; a `device type=bridge, vlan_filtering=1`; or any `bridge-vlan`.
  On AP2 it is hidden (no such section).
- **`tools/bridgevlan.js`:** `MIN_VLAN_ID` 1, `MAX_VLAN_ID` 4094.
  - `findActiveBridge` takes the first `device` section whose VLAN filtering is on and whose member ports are all
    of type switch or ethernet. Filtering is on when `type` is `bridge` and either `vlan_filtering='1'` or a
    `bridge-vlan` names the device.
  - `collectBridgePorts` unions the device's `ports`, the live members and the ports of its `bridge-vlan`s, in
    natural order.
  - `parsePortSpec` matches `^([^:]+)(?::([ut*]*))?$`. `t` means tagged. `u`, or no suffix, means untagged. `*`,
    or no suffix, means PVID.
  - `checkUnsupportedConfig` reports six issues, and any one of them replaces the page with a blocker: a VLAN
    outside 1-4094; an unparsable entry; tagged and PVID together (`t*`); tagged and untagged together (`ut`);
    PVID without traffic (`:*`); and a port untagged in two VLANs.
  - `formatPortSpec` writes a bare name for untagged and `name:t` for tagged; it never writes `*` or `u`.
  - `setBridgeVlanPorts` writes `ports` as a list and unsets it when the list is empty.
  - `parseLocal` treats an absent value as on.
- **`view/network/switch-vlan.js`:**
  - It writes only `network` `bridge-vlan` sections: `device`, `vlan`, `local` and `ports`. A new section gets
    `local` '1' explicitly, and a change writes '0' or '1' explicitly, because netifd's default is true.
  - Making a port untagged in one VLAN removes its untagged entry from the other VLANs and keeps the tagged ones.
  - A tagged assignment on the port's native VLAN is blocked.
  - VLANs can be added (unique id) and removed (after a confirm).
  - Port and VLAN labels go to `/etc/config/luci` (`switchvlan` sections). The page does not check whether an
    interface uses a VLAN.
- **netifd `bridge.c`** (master, fetched 2026-11-15):
  - **Config parse:** a bare entry is untagged with no PVID flag (`port->flags = BRVLAN_F_UNTAGGED`). In a
    suffix, `*` sets PVID and `t` clears untagged; `u` is not parsed (so `:u` is just untagged).
  - **`local`:** true unless the option says otherwise.
  - **Members:** every port a `bridge-vlan` names becomes a bridge member.
  - **Status:** `network.device status` lists `"bridge-vlans": [{"id", "local", "local_pvid", "ports"}]`. Each port
    is printed `"%s%s%s%s"`: the name, then `:` if tagged or PVID, then `t` if tagged, then `*` if PVID. So the
    entries read `name`, `name:t`, `name:*`, `name:t*`.

**D2 details: what the editor reads and the rules it applies**
- **The bridge:** the first `device` section with `type` `bridge`, VLAN filtering on (`vlan_filtering='1'`, or a
  `bridge-vlan` names it), and at least one member port. Every one of its configured members (its `ports` plus
  the ports its `bridge-vlan`s name) must be one of the router's own ports.
  - **Own ports** are the names board.json wires in `network.<role>.ports` and `network.<role>.device`. With no
    board.json, the app cannot tell a port from a virtual device, so it shows the bridge as read only.
- **Rules as LuCI has them:**
  - VLAN ids are 1-4094 and unique.
  - A port is untagged in one VLAN at most. The screen moves the untagged entry the way LuCI does; the server
    refuses a double.
  - `local` is written '0' or '1' explicitly. A new VLAN has `local` on.
  - An empty member list unsets `ports`.
  - Any of LuCI's six unsupported-config issues makes the card read only, with the issues listed. Fix them in
    LuCI's device dialog or the terminal.
- **The app's own:**
  - **(a) Kept text:** an unchanged cell keeps its entry as written. `lan2:u*` stays `lan2:u*`, where LuCI would
    rewrite it to `lan2` on any change in that VLAN.
  - **(b) No renumbering:** an existing VLAN keeps its number. Changing it means a new VLAN and a removal, since an
    interface on `<bridge>.<vid>` would lose its device.
  - **(c) No removing a VLAN in use:** removing a VLAN that an interface uses (`<bridge>.<vid>`, directly or
    through a bridge) is refused, as the swconfig matrix does. LuCI does not check.
  - **(d) Wi-Fi at run time:** ports that Wi-Fi joins at run time (live bridge members that are not own ports) do
    not disqualify the bridge. The card lists them, and they are never edited. LuCI counts live members too.
  - **(e) No double VLAN:** a VLAN configured twice also makes the card read only.
  - Cost if (d) is wrong: the app edits a bridge whose LuCI page shows a blocker. Even then it writes only the
    entries of own ports, and the rollback stands behind every change.

**D3 details: the three layers that protect the app's own path**
- **Layer 1, refused before the preview** (pure rules, Task 2):
  - The app's own VLAN is the one carrying its interface: the interface whose address answers the SSH, on
    `<bridge>.<vid>`, directly or through a bridge.
  - That VLAN cannot be removed (rule c) or renumbered (rule b), and its `local` stays on: the router itself must
    stay in it.
  - **The management-path rule:** the ports that *may carry* the app's SSH are the own VLAN's members with a link,
    or with no link state reported (fail closed). At least one of them must keep its own-VLAN entry exactly as
    written.
    - With the PC on the only linked port, as on the bench, this protects exactly the PC's port.
    - With several linked ports, any one may change, but not all.
  - **Placement unknown:** when the app's interface sits on the bridge itself rather than on a `<bridge>.<vid>`
    device, the app cannot tell which VLAN carries it. The card is then read only with that reason, and the change
    belongs in LuCI.
  - When the app comes in on another interface (on wan, say), there is no own VLAN on this bridge, and only the
    other rules apply.
- **Layer 2, the 60 s rollback** (existing, `ApplyRunner._rpcd_rollback`):
  - The change is staged in an rpcd session and applied with `ubus call uci apply` and a 60 s rollback.
  - The app reconnects with a new SSH connection, sees the change in effect (D4), and only then confirms over that
    connection. A change that cut the PC's path leaves no connection to confirm over, so the router restores itself
    at 60 s.
  - The restore timer is rpcd's, on the router. It runs even if the app, the PC or the network goes away.
  - **What layer 1 lets through:** with two linked ports in the own VLAN, a change can move the PC's own port while
    the other stays. Layer 2 catches it, and Task 6 proves this in `ap5`. The precise own-port lock (the PC's MAC
    in the bridge FDB) is 1.x: standard images have neither `bridge fdb` (`ip-bridge`) nor BusyBox `brctl showmacs`.
- **Layer 3, a rollback that does not bring the router back** (Task 5, and the manual):
  - The app checks 65 s after the change, retrying for 20 s, that the router answers at its old address with its
    previous network config (`restored`).
  - If it does not, the result now adds the recovery steps, on every rollback screen:
    1. Wait until two minutes after the change, then power-cycle the router. After the rollback its flash holds
       the previous settings again. Not before: during the 60 s window, rpcd has the change committed to flash and
       the restore point only in `/var/run`, so a power cut then keeps the change.
    2. If it still does not answer, use failsafe mode (Troubleshooting).
  - If rpcd refuses the apply because another client's rollback is waiting for its confirm (LuCI), nothing is
    staged or applied, and the result says the router refused the change.

**D5 details: `ap5`, "AP5 · DSA image"**
- **Address:** host 192.168.1.7. 192.168.1.6 is ap4; .4 and .5 are the wizard's routers.
- **Seeding:** not seeded by default, like ap4 (`"seed": false`; the `later-*` e2e server seeds `ap4,ap5`).
- **Fixtures:**
  - **board.json** (overlay `ap5.overlay.json`): no `switch` key. `network.lan.ports` is lan1..lan4 and
    `network.wan.device` is `wan`. The model is "Simulated DSA router".
  - **`net/ap5.uci`:** ap4's configs with a DSA network: device `switch` (bridge, ports lan1..lan4,
    `vlan_filtering '1'`); `bridge-vlan` VLAN 1 with lan1..lan4 bare; `bridge-vlan` VLAN 10 with `lan1:t` and
    `lan4:t`, `local '1'`; `lan` on `switch.1` with 192.168.1.7/24; `iot` on `switch.10` with 192.168.10.1/24;
    `wan`/`wan6` on `wan`.
  - **routers.json:** a `dsa` entry `{"ports": [lan1..lan4, wan], "pc_port": "lan3"}`.
  - No `image`: firmware screens do not apply to a made-up board.
- **`SimNetwork`:**
  - **DSA port devices:** `network.device status` reports the port devices with `carrier` (lan3, plus any port in
    `dsa_links`), and the bridge with `bridge-members` and netifd-format `bridge-vlans`.
  - **Reachability:** the PC reaches lan only while lan3 is in lan's bridge VLAN without `t` and that VLAN has
    `local` on. So a wrong change really cuts the app off until rpcd's rollback (the existing staged apply)
    restores it.
  - `pending_rollback()` stands in for another client's apply that is waiting for its confirm.

**Deferred minors from v1.0a2, for v1.0b (D8):**
1. The Bench Guard tests cover one service call per write, not the HTTP 423 on every apply endpoint. The new bridge
   endpoints get theirs in Task 6.
2. `on_applied` is not called from `apply_access` or the Wi-Fi apply, so those leave the LuCI page list stale for

3. The plugin header regex is not anchored to `baseclass.extend(`.
4. A header-less plugin row's group is not fullmatched before it reaches a uci path.
5. Reset counters' proof can say "not verified" after two resets in a row, and ip6tables is not checked.
6. Unreadable iptables lines are dropped without a note.
7. The iptables tab reads all eight listings every 10 s.
8. A negative `delay_root` is accepted.

## Review Focus

The five input classes and failure modes the spec implies that are most likely to bite. Each has its test in the
owning task.
1. **A change layer 1 allows that still cuts the PC off.** With the PC on lan3 and another linked port in VLAN 1,
   moving lan3 out of VLAN 1 passes the rules. The router must come back by itself: `reconnected` false,
   `restored` true, and VLAN 1 still holds lan3 (Task 6,
   `test_a_change_that_moves_the_pcs_port_rolls_back_by_itself`).
2. **Entries the user did not touch.** `lan2:u*`, `lan3:*` and an entry order that is not natural must survive an
   edit of another cell in the same VLAN unchanged. A VLAN no change touches must give no ops at all (Task 2,
   `test_an_unchanged_cell_keeps_its_entry_as_written` and `test_no_change_writes_nothing`).
3. **netifd's report with Wi-Fi in the bridge.** A `bridge-vlans` entry may list `wlan0` (a hotplug member) and
   carry `local_pvid`. The verify must count own ports only, and a `bridge-members` list with `wlan0` must not hide
   the bridge (Task 1, `test_live_vlans_count_own_ports_only`; Task 4,
   `test_wifi_at_run_time_is_listed_not_edited`).
4. **Where the app's interface sits.** The own VLAN must be found whether lan is on `switch.1`, on a bridge
   `br-lan` whose port is `switch.1`, on `switch` itself (read only), or on wan (no own VLAN) (Task 4,
   `test_the_own_vlan_wherever_lan_sits`).
5. **Another client's rollback is pending.** rpcd refuses a second rollback apply ("Permission denied"). The
   result must say the router refused the change, with nothing staged left behind and the VLANs unchanged
   (Task 6, `test_a_pending_rollback_of_another_client_refuses_the_change`).

```python
"""DSA bridge VLANs (v1.0a3): what LuCI's Switch/VLAN config page reads (`view/network/switch-vlan.js` and
`tools/bridgevlan.js`), as pure functions, what netifd reports for a bridge's VLANs (`bridge.c`), and the state the
editor shows."""

from __future__ import annotations

import json
import re
from collections.abc import Mapping, Sequence
from dataclasses import dataclass
from typing import Literal

from openwrtgui.net.parse import options, sections

Cell = Literal["u", "t"]
MIN_VLAN = 1
MAX_VLAN = 4094
_SPEC = re.compile(r"([^:]+)(?::([ut*]*))?")  # LuCI's parsePortSpec
_VID = re.compile(r"[0-9]{1,4}")
_OFF = frozenset({"0", "false", "no", "off", "disabled"})


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class PortSpec:
    """One `ports` entry as LuCI reads it: a bare name is untagged with PVID."""

    port: str
    tagged: bool
    untagged: bool
    pvid: bool


def parse_spec(text: str) -> PortSpec | None:
    m = _SPEC.fullmatch(text)
    if m is None:
        return None
    flags = m.group(2) or ""
    return PortSpec(m.group(1), "t" in flags, "u" in flags or not flags, "*" in flags or not flags)


def render_spec(port: str, cell: Cell) -> str:
    """LuCI's formatPortSpec: untagged (native) is the bare name, tagged is `name:t`."""
    return f"{port}:t" if cell == "t" else port


def cell_of(spec: PortSpec) -> Cell:
    return "t" if spec.tagged else "u"


def netifd_spec(text: str) -> str | None:
    """The entry as netifd prints it in `network.device status` (bridge.c, bridge_dump_port). Its config parser
    knows `t` and `*` only, so a bare name and `:u` both print `name`, and `:*` and `:u*` both print `name:*`."""
    m = _SPEC.fullmatch(text)
    if m is None:
        return None
    flags = m.group(2) or ""
    tagged, star = "t" in flags, "*" in flags
    return f"{m.group(1)}{':' if tagged or star else ''}{'t' if tagged else ''}{'*' if star else ''}"


def natural(name: str) -> tuple[str | int, ...]:
    """LuCI's naturalCompare order: lan2 before lan10. Digit runs sit at odd positions, so types line up."""
    return tuple(int(part) if i % 2 else part for i, part in enumerate(re.split(r"(\d+)", name)))


def parse_local(value: str | None) -> bool:
    """LuCI's parseLocal: absent is on, which is netifd's default."""
    return not value or value.strip().lower() not in _OFF


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class BridgeDevice:
    section: str
    name: str
    ports: tuple[str, ...]
    vlan_filtering: bool


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class BridgeVlanConf:
    section: str
    device: str
    vlan: int | None  # None: not a VLAN number from 1 to 4094
    raw_vlan: str
    local: bool
    entries: tuple[str, ...]  # `ports` as written


def bridge_devices(uci: Mapping[str, str]) -> list[BridgeDevice]:
    out = []
    for section in sections(uci, "network", "device"):
        o = options(uci, "network", section)
        if o.get("type") == "bridge":
            ports = tuple(o.get("ports", "").split())
            out.append(BridgeDevice(section, o.get("name", ""), ports, o.get("vlan_filtering") == "1"))
    return out


def _vid(raw: str) -> int | None:
    return int(raw) if _VID.fullmatch(raw) and MIN_VLAN <= int(raw) <= MAX_VLAN else None


def bridge_vlans(uci: Mapping[str, str]) -> list[BridgeVlanConf]:
    out = []
    for section in sections(uci, "network", "bridge-vlan"):
        o = options(uci, "network", section)
        raw = o.get("vlan", "")
        entries = tuple(o.get("ports", "").split())
        out.append(BridgeVlanConf(section, o.get("device", ""), _vid(raw), raw, parse_local(o.get("local")), entries))
    return out


def members(device: BridgeDevice, vlans: Sequence[BridgeVlanConf]) -> tuple[str, ...]:
    """The bridge's configured ports and every port its bridge-vlans name (netifd adds those as members)."""
    named = {p.port for v in vlans if v.device == device.name for p in map(parse_spec, v.entries) if p}
    return tuple(sorted({*device.ports, *named}, key=natural))


def _filtering(device: BridgeDevice, vlans: Sequence[BridgeVlanConf]) -> bool:
    """LuCI's isVlanFilteringEnabled: vlan_filtering='1', or a bridge-vlan names the bridge."""
    return device.vlan_filtering or any(v.device == device.name for v in vlans)


def find_active_bridge(
    devices: Sequence[BridgeDevice], vlans: Sequence[BridgeVlanConf], physical: frozenset[str]
) -> BridgeDevice | None:
    """LuCI's findActiveBridge: the first bridge with VLAN filtering whose members are all the router's own ports.
    Members Wi-Fi adds at run time are not counted (D2 d)."""
    for device in devices:
        ports = members(device, vlans)
        if _filtering(device, vlans) and ports and all(p in physical for p in ports):
            return device
    return None


def _entry_issue(vlan: int, text: str, spec: PortSpec | None) -> str | None:
    if spec is None:
        return f"VLAN {vlan}: {text!r} is not a port entry"
    if spec.tagged and spec.pvid:
        return f"VLAN {vlan}: {spec.port} is tagged and PVID at once ({text})"
    if spec.tagged and spec.untagged:
        return f"VLAN {vlan}: {spec.port} is tagged and untagged at once ({text})"
    if not spec.tagged and not spec.untagged:
        return f"VLAN {vlan}: {spec.port} has a PVID but no traffic ({text})"
    return None


def unsupported(bridge: str, vlans: Sequence[BridgeVlanConf]) -> list[str]:
    """LuCI's checkUnsupportedConfig, plus a VLAN configured twice (D2 e): any one keeps the card read only."""
    issues: list[str] = []
    untagged: dict[str, int] = {}
    seen: set[int] = set()
    for v in (v for v in vlans if v.device == bridge):
        if v.vlan is None:
            issues.append(f"VLAN {v.raw_vlan!r} is not a VLAN number from {MIN_VLAN} to {MAX_VLAN}")
            continue
        if v.vlan in seen:
            issues.append(f"VLAN {v.vlan} is configured twice")
        seen.add(v.vlan)
        for text in v.entries:
            spec = parse_spec(text)
            issue = _entry_issue(v.vlan, text, spec)
            if issue is not None:
                issues.append(issue)
            elif spec is not None and spec.untagged:
                if spec.port in untagged:
                    issues.append(f"{spec.port} is untagged in VLAN {untagged[spec.port]} and VLAN {v.vlan}")
                else:
                    untagged[spec.port] = v.vlan
    return issues


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class LiveVlan:
    """One VLAN as netifd reports it: the verify compares these with what the change asks (D4)."""

    vlan: int
    local: bool
    ports: frozenset[str]  # netifd's entries (`lan1`, `lan1:t`, `lan1:*`), own ports only


def _port_name(entry: str) -> str:
    return entry.split(":", 1)[0]


def live_vlans(status_json: str, bridge: str, ports: frozenset[str]) -> tuple[LiveVlan, ...]:
    """`ubus call network.device status`: the bridge's `bridge-vlans`, counting the editor's own ports only."""
    try:
        status = json.loads(status_json)
    except ValueError:
        return ()
    entry = status.get(bridge) if isinstance(status, dict) else None
    listed = entry.get("bridge-vlans") if isinstance(entry, dict) else None
    if not isinstance(listed, list):
        return ()
    out = []
    for v in listed:
        if isinstance(v, dict) and isinstance(v.get("id"), int):
            entries = [p for p in v.get("ports", []) if isinstance(p, str) and _port_name(p) in ports]
            out.append(LiveVlan(v["id"], bool(v.get("local", True)), frozenset(entries)))
    return tuple(sorted(out, key=lambda x: x.vlan))


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class BridgePortView:
    name: str
    link: bool | None  # None: netifd reports no carrier for the port
    may_carry: bool  # D3: a member of the app's own VLAN with a link, or with no link state (fail closed)


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class BridgeVlanView:
    section: str
    vlan: int
    local: bool
    members: dict[str, Cell]
    entries: dict[str, str]  # port -> its entry as written; an unchanged cell keeps it (D2 a)
    used_by: tuple[str, ...]  # interfaces on <bridge>.<vid>, directly or through a bridge
    own: bool  # carries the app's interface


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class BridgeState:
    router_id: str
    bridge: str | None  # None: no VLAN-filtering bridge of the router's own ports
    editable: bool
    blocker: str | None  # why the card is read only, or why there is no bridge to show
    issues: tuple[str, ...]  # LuCI's unsupported-config issues
    ports: tuple[BridgePortView, ...]
    vlans: tuple[BridgeVlanView, ...]
    own_interface: str | None
    own_vlan: int | None
    runtime: tuple[str, ...] = ()  # members Wi-Fi joins at run time: listed, never edited (D2 d)
```

```python
"""The VLAN editor's rules (v1.0a3 D2, D3 layer 1) and its uci ops. Only bridge-vlan sections are written: never a
device's ports or vlan_filtering, never an interface."""

from __future__ import annotations

import re
from collections.abc import Mapping, Sequence
from dataclasses import dataclass

from openwrtgui.ports.bridge import (
    MAX_VLAN,
    MIN_VLAN,
    BridgeState,
    BridgeVlanView,
    Cell,
    LiveVlan,
    natural,
    netifd_spec,
    parse_spec,
    render_spec,
)
from openwrtgui.uci import UciOp

_PORT = re.compile(r"[A-Za-z0-9_.@-]{1,15}")  # an interface name (IFNAMSIZ): it reaches a uci value


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class BridgeVlanRequest:
    section: str | None  # None: a new VLAN
    vlan: int
    members: Mapping[str, Cell]
    local: bool = True


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class BridgeChange:
    ops: tuple[UciOp, ...]
    warnings: tuple[str, ...]
    want: tuple[LiveVlan, ...]  # every VLAN of the bridge as netifd should report it after the change


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class _Final:
    local: bool
    entries: dict[str, str]  # port -> entry


def _editable(state: BridgeState) -> str:
    if state.bridge is None:
        raise ValueError("This router has no bridge with VLAN filtering")
    if not state.editable:
        raise ValueError(state.blocker or f"The VLANs of {state.bridge} are read only here")
    return state.bridge


def _deletable(by_section: Mapping[str, BridgeVlanView], section: str, bridge: str) -> None:
    v = by_section.get(section)
    if v is None:
        raise ValueError(f"There is no VLAN {section}")
    if v.used_by:  # D2 c: LuCI does not check
        iface = v.used_by[0]
        raise ValueError(f"VLAN {v.vlan} carries {iface} ({bridge}.{v.vlan}). Move {iface} to another device first.")


def _checked(
    req: BridgeVlanRequest, by_section: Mapping[str, BridgeVlanView], final: Mapping[int, _Final], own: str | None
) -> BridgeVlanView | None:
    old = by_section.get(req.section) if req.section is not None else None
    if req.section is not None and old is None:
        raise ValueError(f"There is no VLAN {req.section}")
    if old is not None and old.vlan != req.vlan:  # D2 b
        raise ValueError(
            f"VLAN {old.vlan} keeps its number here: add VLAN {req.vlan} and remove VLAN {old.vlan} instead."
        )
    if not MIN_VLAN <= req.vlan <= MAX_VLAN:
        raise ValueError(f"VLAN numbers are {MIN_VLAN} to {MAX_VLAN}")
    if req.vlan in final:
        raise ValueError(f"There is already a VLAN {req.vlan}")
    if old is not None and old.own and not req.local:  # D3 layer 1
        raise ValueError(
            f"VLAN {old.vlan} carries {own}, this app's connection: the router stays in it, so local stays on."
        )
    return old


def _entries(req: BridgeVlanRequest, old: BridgeVlanView | None, ports: set[str], bridge: str) -> dict[str, str]:
    """The request's members as entries: a cell the user did not change keeps its text (D2 a)."""
    out: dict[str, str] = {}
    for port, cell in req.members.items():
        if port not in ports or not _PORT.fullmatch(port):
            raise ValueError(f"{port} is not a port of {bridge}")
        same = old is not None and old.members.get(port) == cell
        out[port] = old.entries[port] if same and old is not None else render_spec(port, cell)
    return out


def _upsert_ops(n: int, req: BridgeVlanRequest, old: BridgeVlanView | None, entries: Mapping[str, str], bridge: str) -> list[UciOp]:
    listed = sorted(entries.values(), key=natural)  # LuCI sorts the entries
    local = "1" if req.local else "0"
    if old is None:
        p = f"network.$b{n}"
        return [
            UciOp("add", p, "bridge-vlan"),
            UciOp("set", f"{p}.device", bridge),
            UciOp("set", f"{p}.vlan", str(req.vlan)),
            UciOp("set", f"{p}.local", local),
            *(UciOp("add_list", f"{p}.ports", e) for e in listed),
        ]
    p = f"network.{old.section}"
    ops = [UciOp("set", f"{p}.local", local)] if req.local != old.local else []
    if listed != sorted(old.entries.values(), key=natural):
        ops += [UciOp("delete", f"{p}.ports"), *(UciOp("add_list", f"{p}.ports", e) for e in listed)]
    return ops


def _warnings(req: BridgeVlanRequest, old: BridgeVlanView | None, entries: Mapping[str, str], own: str | None) -> list[str]:
    out = [f"VLAN {req.vlan} has no ports."] if not entries else []
    if old is not None and old.own:
        out.append(f"VLAN {req.vlan} carries {own}, this app's connection; the change applies with a 60 s rollback.")
    if old is not None and old.local and not req.local:
        out += [
            f"VLAN {req.vlan} carries {iface}: with local off the router leaves VLAN {req.vlan}, so {iface} gets no traffic."
            for iface in old.used_by
        ]
    return out


def _untagged_once(final: Mapping[int, _Final]) -> None:
    seen: dict[str, int] = {}
    for vlan, f in sorted(final.items()):
        for spec in filter(None, map(parse_spec, f.entries.values())):
            if not spec.untagged:
                continue
            if spec.port in seen:
                raise ValueError(
                    f"{spec.port} is untagged in VLAN {seen[spec.port]} and VLAN {vlan}; "
                    "a port is untagged in one VLAN at most."
                )
            seen[spec.port] = vlan


def _own_path_kept(state: BridgeState, final: Mapping[int, _Final], router_name: str) -> None:
    """D3 layer 1, the management-path rule. The app cannot tell which port is the PC's (the own-port lock by the
    bridge FDB is 1.x), so the rollback catches a change to the PC's port while another linked port stays."""
    if state.own_vlan is None:
        return
    carry = [p.name for p in state.ports if p.may_carry]
    before = next((v for v in state.vlans if v.vlan == state.own_vlan), None)
    if not carry or before is None:
        return
    after = final.get(state.own_vlan)
    kept = [p for p in carry if after is not None and after.entries.get(p) == before.entries.get(p)]
    if not kept:
        raise ValueError(
            f"{', '.join(carry)} may carry this app's SSH to {router_name} (the ports with a link in "
            f"VLAN {state.own_vlan}, which carries {state.own_interface}): one of them keeps its "
            f"VLAN {state.own_vlan} membership as it is."
        )


def _want(final: Mapping[int, _Final]) -> tuple[LiveVlan, ...]:
    return tuple(
        LiveVlan(vlan, f.local, frozenset(filter(None, map(netifd_spec, f.entries.values()))))
        for vlan, f in sorted(final.items())
    )


def bridge_change(
    state: BridgeState, upserts: Sequence[BridgeVlanRequest], deletes: Sequence[str], router_name: str
) -> BridgeChange:
    bridge = _editable(state)
    by_section = {v.section: v for v in state.vlans}
    for section in deletes:
        _deletable(by_section, section, bridge)
    gone = {u.section for u in upserts if u.section} | set(deletes)
    final = {v.vlan: _Final(v.local, dict(v.entries)) for v in state.vlans if v.section not in gone}
    ports = {p.name for p in state.ports}
    ops: list[UciOp] = []
    warnings: list[str] = []
    for n, req in enumerate(upserts, 1):
        old = _checked(req, by_section, final, state.own_interface)
        entries = _entries(req, old, ports, bridge)
        final[req.vlan] = _Final(req.local, entries)
        ops += _upsert_ops(n, req, old, entries, bridge)
        warnings += _warnings(req, old, entries, state.own_interface)
    _untagged_once(final)
    _own_path_kept(state, final, router_name)
    ops += [UciOp("delete", f"network.{s}") for s in deletes]
    return BridgeChange(tuple(ops), tuple(warnings), _want(final))
```

```python
    def _dsa_reachable(self, lan: Any) -> bool:
        """DSA (v1.0a3 ap5): lan sits on <bridge>.<vid>; the PC's port must be in that bridge VLAN without `t`,
        and the router itself in it (`local`)."""
        bridge, _, vid = (lan.device or "").partition(".")
        if not vid.isdigit():
            return False
```

```python
"""The VLAN editor's state (v1.0a3): the active bridge as LuCI picks it, its VLANs, the router's own ports from
board.json, their link from netifd, and the VLAN that carries the app's own interface (D3)."""

from __future__ import annotations

import json
from collections.abc import Mapping, Sequence
from typing import Any

from openwrtgui.net import commands as c
from openwrtgui.net.state import NetworkState
from openwrtgui.ports.bridge import (

    BridgePortView,
    BridgeState,

    BridgeVlanView,

    cell_of,

    natural,
    parse_spec,
    unsupported,
)

from openwrtgui.store.routers import RouterRecord
from openwrtgui.transport.base import Transport

UNSUPPORTED = (
    "LuCI's Switch/VLAN page does not support this configuration either: fix it in LuCI's device dialog or the "
    "terminal."
)


def board_ports(desc: SelfDescription) -> frozenset[str]:
    """The router's own ports, as board.json wires them (`network.<role>.ports` and `.device`)."""
    board = desc.board
    out: set[str] = set()
    for role in (board.default_network if board else {}).values():
        if isinstance(role, dict):
            out |= {p for p in role.get("ports", []) if isinstance(p, str)}
            if isinstance(role.get("device"), str):
                out.add(role["device"])
    return frozenset(out)


async def _status(t: Transport) -> dict[str, Any]:
    r = await t.run(c.DEVICE_STATUS)
    try:
        value = json.loads(r.stdout) if r.ok and r.stdout.strip() else {}
    except ValueError:
        return {}
    return value if isinstance(value, dict) else {}


def _expanded(names: Sequence[str], net: NetworkState) -> list[str]:
    """Devices and, one hop down, the ports of the bridges among them (a legacy `ifname` lists several)."""
    bridges = {d.name: d for d in net.devices if d.type == "bridge"}
    return [*names, *(p for n in names if n in bridges for p in bridges[n].ports)]


def _used_by(vlan_device: str, net: NetworkState) -> tuple[str, ...]:
    return tuple(
        v.conf.name
        for v in net.interfaces
        if v.conf is not None and vlan_device in _expanded((v.conf.device or "").split(), net)
    )


def _own_vlan(bridge: str, net: NetworkState) -> tuple[int | None, str | None]:
    own = net.conf(net.own_interface) if net.own_interface else None
    if own is None or not own.device:
        return None, None
    names = _expanded(own.device.split(), net)
    if bridge in names:
        return None, (
            f"{own.name} uses {bridge} itself, so the app cannot tell which VLAN carries its connection: "
            "edit these VLANs in LuCI."
        )
    for name in names:
        head, _, vid = name.partition(".")
        if head == bridge and vid.isdigit():
            return int(vid), None
    return None, None


def _views(bridge: str, vlans: Sequence[BridgeVlanConf], net: NetworkState, own_vlan: int | None) -> list[BridgeVlanView]:
    out = []
    for v in vlans:
        if v.device != bridge or v.vlan is None:
            continue
        parsed = [(text, parse_spec(text)) for text in v.entries]
        cells = {s.port: cell_of(s) for _, s in parsed if s}
        entries = {s.port: text for text, s in parsed if s}
        used = _used_by(f"{bridge}.{v.vlan}", net)
        out.append(BridgeVlanView(v.section, v.vlan, v.local, cells, entries, used, v.vlan == own_vlan))
    return sorted(out, key=lambda x: x.vlan)


def _absent(rec: RouterRecord, net: NetworkState, devices: Sequence[BridgeDevice], vlans: Sequence[BridgeVlanConf], physical: frozenset[str]) -> BridgeState:
    filtering = [d for d in devices if d.vlan_filtering or any(v.device == d.name for v in vlans)]
    blocker = None
    if filtering and not physical:
        blocker = (
            f"{filtering[0].name} has members the app cannot tell from virtual devices (board.json lists no ports), "
            "so it shows no VLAN editor for it."
        )
    elif filtering:
        blocker = (
            f"{filtering[0].name} has members that are not ports of this router, so it shows no VLAN editor "
            "for it (LuCI shows none either)."
        )
    return BridgeState(rec.id, None, False, blocker, (), (), (), net.own_interface, None)


def _ports(names: Sequence[str], status: Mapping[str, Any], own: BridgeVlanView | None) -> tuple[BridgePortView, ...]:
    out = []
    for name in names:
        entry = status.get(name)
        link = entry.get("carrier") if isinstance(entry, dict) and isinstance(entry.get("carrier"), bool) else None
        out.append(BridgePortView(name, link, own is not None and name in own.members and link is not False))
    return tuple(out)


async def read_bridge(
    t: Transport, rec: RouterRecord, desc: SelfDescription, net: NetworkState, uci: Mapping[str, str]
) -> BridgeState:
    physical = board_ports(desc)
    devices, vlans = bridge_devices(uci), bridge_vlans(uci)
    active = find_active_bridge(devices, vlans, physical)
    if active is None:
        return _absent(rec, net, devices, vlans, physical)
    status = await _status(t)
    entry = status.get(active.name)
    live = entry.get("bridge-members", []) if isinstance(entry, dict) else []
    runtime = tuple(sorted((m for m in live if isinstance(m, str) and m not in physical), key=natural))
    issues = tuple(unsupported(active.name, vlans))
    own_vlan, placement = _own_vlan(active.name, net)
    views = _views(active.name, vlans, net, own_vlan)
    own = next((v for v in views if v.own), None)
    if own is None:
        own_vlan = None
    blocker = UNSUPPORTED if issues else placement
    return BridgeState(
        router_id=rec.id,
        bridge=active.name,
        editable=blocker is None,
        blocker=blocker,
        issues=issues,
        ports=_ports(members(active, vlans), status, own),
        vlans=tuple(views),
        own_interface=net.own_interface,
        own_vlan=own_vlan,
        runtime=runtime,
    )
```

```python
"""The VLAN editor on a DSA router (v1.0a3): state, exact previews, applies with the 60 s rollback (D3 layer 2),
proof by netifd's own report of the bridge's VLANs (D4)."""

from __future__ import annotations

import asyncio
from collections.abc import Awaitable, Callable, Mapping, MutableMapping, Sequence
from typing import Any

from openwrtgui.net import commands as c
from openwrtgui.net.apply import (
    ApplyRunner,
    NetApplyResult,
    NetCheck,
    NetPlan,
    Planned,
    net_plan,
    rollback_warning,
)
from openwrtgui.net.service import network_snapshot
from openwrtgui.net.state import read_network
from openwrtgui.ports.bridge import BridgeState, LiveVlan, live_vlans
from openwrtgui.ports.bridge_rules import BridgeChange, BridgeVlanRequest, bridge_change
from openwrtgui.ports.bridge_state import read_bridge
from openwrtgui.probe.describe import SelfDescription, describe_router
from openwrtgui.store.routers import RouterRecord, RouterStore
from openwrtgui.transport.base import Transport
from openwrtgui.transport.pool import ConnectionPool

SCREEN = "Ports"
SETTLE_S = 10.0
POLL_S = 1.0


def _differs(want: Sequence[LiveVlan], now: Sequence[LiveVlan]) -> str:
    asked, got = {v.vlan: v for v in want}, {v.vlan: v for v in now}
    ids = sorted(v for v in {*asked, *got} if asked.get(v) != got.get(v))
    return ", ".join(f"VLAN {v}" for v in ids) or "no VLANs reported"


class BridgeVlanService:
    def __init__(
        self,
        *,
        pool: ConnectionPool,
        routers: RouterStore,
        descriptions: MutableMapping[str, SelfDescription],
        runner: ApplyRunner,
        sleep: Callable[[float], Awaitable[Any]] = asyncio.sleep,
        settle_s: float = SETTLE_S,
        poll_s: float = POLL_S,
    ) -> None:
        self._pool = pool
        self._routers = routers
        self._descriptions = descriptions
        self._runner = runner
        self._sleep = sleep
        self._settle_s = settle_s
        self._poll_s = poll_s

    def _record(self, router_id: str) -> RouterRecord:
        rec = self._routers.get(router_id)
        if rec is None:
            raise ValueError(f"Unknown router {router_id}")
        return rec

    async def _read_on(self, rec: RouterRecord, t: Transport) -> tuple[BridgeState, dict[str, str]]:
        desc = self._descriptions.get(rec.id)
        if desc is None:
            desc = await describe_router(t)
            self._descriptions[rec.id] = desc
        net, uci = await read_network(t, rec.id, desc)
        return await read_bridge(t, rec, desc, net, uci), uci

    async def state(self, router_id: str) -> BridgeState:
        rec = self._record(router_id)
        return (await self._read_on(rec, await self._pool.get(rec)))[0]

    def _plan(
        self,
        rec: RouterRecord,
        state: BridgeState,
        uci: Mapping[str, str],
        upserts: Sequence[BridgeVlanRequest],
        deletes: Sequence[str],
    ) -> tuple[NetPlan, BridgeChange]:
        change = bridge_change(state, upserts, deletes, rec.name)
        plan = net_plan(
            router_id=rec.id,
            screen=SCREEN,
            config="network",
            ops=change.ops,
            current=uci,
            rollback=True,
            warnings=[*change.warnings, rollback_warning(rec.name, rec.host)],
        )
        return plan, change

    async def plan(
        self, router_id: str, upserts: Sequence[BridgeVlanRequest], deletes: Sequence[str]
    ) -> NetPlan:
        rec = self._record(router_id)
        state, uci = await self._read_on(rec, await self._pool.get(rec))
        return self._plan(rec, state, uci, upserts, deletes)[0]

    async def apply(
        self,
        router_id: str,
        upserts: Sequence[BridgeVlanRequest],
        deletes: Sequence[str],
        digest: str | None = None,
    ) -> NetApplyResult:
        rec = self._record(router_id)

        async def make(t: Transport) -> Planned:
            state, uci = await self._read_on(rec, t)
            plan, change = self._plan(rec, state, uci, upserts, deletes)
            assert state.bridge is not None  # bridge_change refuses a router without one
            ports = frozenset(p.name for p in state.ports)
            return Planned(plan, self._verify(state.bridge, ports, change.want), network_snapshot(uci))

        return await self._runner.run_planned(rec, make, digest)

    def _verify(
        self, bridge: str, ports: frozenset[str], want: tuple[LiveVlan, ...]
    ) -> Callable[[Transport], Awaitable[list[NetCheck]]]:
        async def live(t2: Transport) -> tuple[LiveVlan, ...]:
            r = await t2.run(c.DEVICE_STATUS)
            return live_vlans(r.stdout if r.ok else "", bridge, ports)

        async def verify(t2: Transport) -> list[NetCheck]:
            now = await live(t2)
            for _ in range(int(self._settle_s / self._poll_s)):
                if now == want:
                    break
                await self._sleep(self._poll_s)
                now = await live(t2)
            if now == want:
                return [NetCheck("bridge", True, f"{bridge} has the VLANs as asked")]
            return [NetCheck("bridge", False, f"{bridge} differs: {_differs(want, now)}")]

        return verify
```

```python
"""The VLAN editor on a DSA router (v1.0a3 D6): LuCI's Switch/VLAN config."""

from __future__ import annotations

from typing import Annotated, Literal

from fastapi import APIRouter, Depends, HTTPException
from pydantic import BaseModel

from openwrtgui.api.deps import get_ctx, require_token
from openwrtgui.api.net_api import Digest, NetPlanOut
from openwrtgui.context import AppContext
from openwrtgui.net.apply import NetApplyResult
from openwrtgui.ports.bridge import BridgeState
from openwrtgui.ports.bridge_rules import BridgeVlanRequest

router = APIRouter(
    prefix="/api/routers/{router_id}/ports/bridge", dependencies=[Depends(require_token)], tags=["ports"]
)
Ctx = Annotated[AppContext, Depends(get_ctx)]


class BridgeVlanIn(BaseModel):
    section: str | None = None
    vlan: int
    members: dict[str, Literal["u", "t"]]
    local: bool = True


class BridgeVlansIn(BaseModel):
    upserts: list[BridgeVlanIn] = []
    deletes: list[str] = []

    def upserts_domain(self) -> list[BridgeVlanRequest]:
        return [BridgeVlanRequest(v.section, v.vlan, dict(v.members), v.local) for v in self.upserts]


class BridgeVlansApplyIn(BridgeVlansIn, Digest):
    pass


def _known(ctx: AppContext, router_id: str) -> str:
    if ctx.routers.get(router_id) is None:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail=f"No router {router_id}")
    return router_id


@router.get("")
async def bridge_state(router_id: str, ctx: Ctx) -> BridgeState:
    return await ctx.bridge_vlans.state(_known(ctx, router_id))


@router.post("/preview")
async def bridge_preview(router_id: str, body: BridgeVlansIn, ctx: Ctx) -> NetPlanOut:
    plan = await ctx.bridge_vlans.plan(_known(ctx, router_id), body.upserts_domain(), body.deletes)
    return NetPlanOut.of(plan)


@router.post("/apply")
async def bridge_apply(router_id: str, body: BridgeVlansApplyIn, ctx: Ctx) -> NetApplyResult:
    return await ctx.bridge_vlans.apply(_known(ctx, router_id), body.upserts_domain(), body.deletes, body.digest)
```

```ts
import type { BridgeState, BridgeVlanIn, BridgeVlansIn } from '../../api/types'
import type { Cell } from './vlan'

/** One VLAN of the bridge as the card edits it (v1.0a3): a cell per port, the local switch. */
export type BridgeRow = { key: string; section: string | null; vlan: number; local: boolean; cells: Record<string, Cell>; deleted: boolean }

const MAX_VLAN = 4094

function emptyCells(state: BridgeState): Record<string, Cell> {
  return Object.fromEntries(state.ports.map((p) => [p.name, '-' as Cell]))
}

export function bridgeDraft(state: BridgeState): BridgeRow[] {
  return state.vlans.map((v) => ({ key: v.section, section: v.section, vlan: v.vlan, local: v.local, cells: { ...emptyCells(state), ...v.members }, deleted: false }))
}

/** LuCI's move: a port made untagged in one VLAN leaves the VLAN it was untagged in; tagged memberships stay. */
export function setCell(rows: BridgeRow[], key: string, port: string, cell: Cell): BridgeRow[] {
  return rows.map((r) => {
    if (r.key === key) return { ...r, cells: { ...r.cells, [port]: cell } }
    if (cell === 'u' && !r.deleted && r.cells[port] === 'u') return { ...r, cells: { ...r.cells, [port]: '-' } }
    return r
  })
}

/** A new VLAN with the lowest number no row uses, local on, no ports yet. */
export function newBridgeVlan(state: BridgeState, rows: BridgeRow[]): BridgeRow {
  const used = new Set(rows.map((r) => r.vlan))
  let vlan = 1
  while (used.has(vlan) && vlan < MAX_VLAN) vlan += 1
  return { key: `new-${rows.length}-${vlan}`, section: null, vlan, local: true, cells: emptyCells(state), deleted: false }
}

function members(cells: Record<string, Cell>): BridgeVlanIn['members'] {
  return Object.fromEntries(Object.entries(cells).filter(([, c]) => c !== '-')) as BridgeVlanIn['members']
}

const same = (a: BridgeVlanIn['members'], b: BridgeVlanIn['members']): boolean =>
  JSON.stringify(Object.entries(a).sort()) === JSON.stringify(Object.entries(b).sort())

/** The changed VLANs, each with its whole member list, and the deleted ones; null when nothing changed. */
export function bridgeRequest(state: BridgeState, rows: BridgeRow[]): BridgeVlansIn | null {
  const upserts: BridgeVlanIn[] = []
  const deletes: string[] = []
  for (const row of rows) {
    if (row.deleted) {
      if (row.section) deletes.push(row.section)
      continue
    }
    const old = state.vlans.find((v) => v.section === row.section)
    const now = members(row.cells)
    if (old && old.local === row.local && same(old.members, now)) continue
    upserts.push({ ...(row.section ? { section: row.section } : {}), vlan: row.vlan, members: now, local: row.local })
  }
  return upserts.length || deletes.length ? { upserts, deletes } : null
}
```

`docs/manual/Ports-and-VLANs.md`, under the `## VLANs on a DSA router` heading and paragraph that Task 8 added:

```markdown
- Each row is a VLAN, and each column is one of the router's own ports, as board.json lists them. Click a cell to
  go from **—** (not a member) to **U** (untagged, the port's native VLAN) to **T** (tagged).
- Making a port untagged in one VLAN takes it out of the VLAN it was untagged in, as LuCI does. Its tagged
  memberships stay.
- **Local** means the router itself is in the VLAN. A new VLAN has it on.
- **+ Add VLAN** adds a VLAN with the lowest free number (1-4094). **Delete** removes a VLAN that no interface uses.
- An existing VLAN keeps its number. To renumber it, add the new VLAN and remove the old one.
- An entry you did not change is written back as it was. LuCI rewrites, for example, `lan2:u*` as `lan2`.
- When LuCI's own page would refuse the configuration (a VLAN number out of range, an entry such as `lan1:t*`, a
  port untagged in two VLANs), the card lists the reasons and stays read only. Fix those in LuCI's device dialog or
  the terminal.
- Ports that Wi-Fi joins at run time are listed under the matrix and never edited.
- Port on/off, link state and mirroring on a DSA router are not here yet. LuCI's port and VLAN labels are left to
  LuCI.

**How the app keeps its own connection.** A VLAN change can cut the app off its router. Three things stand in the
way:

1. **Refused before the preview.**
   - The VLAN that carries the app's connection (the interface its SSH comes in on, on `switch.<vid>`) cannot be
     deleted or renumbered, and its **Local** stays on.
   - The ports with a link in that VLAN *may carry this app's SSH* (marked SSH in the header). At least one of them
     must keep its membership in that VLAN as it is.
   - When the interface sits on the bridge itself, the app cannot tell which VLAN carries it, so the card is read
     only.
2. **The 60 s rollback.**
   - Every change applies with a 60 s rollback, as LuCI's Save & Apply does.
   - The app reconnects over a new connection and checks netifd's own report of the bridge's VLANs. Only then does
     it confirm.
   - If the change cut the app off, nothing confirms it, and the router restores its previous network settings by
     itself 60 s after the change. This happens even if the app or the PC is gone.
3. **If the router does not come back.** The app checks at 65 s that the router answers again with its previous
   settings. If it does not:
   - Wait until **2 minutes** after the change, then **power-cycle** the router. After the rollback its flash holds
     the previous settings again.
   - Do not power-cycle sooner: during the 60 s window the flash holds the change.
   - If the router still does not answer, see Troubleshooting.

The app cannot yet tell which port is your PC's, because standard images cannot list the bridge's address table.
So with two linked ports in its VLAN, it lets you move either one, and if you move your own, the rollback brings
```

```markdown
## A router that does not come back after a rollback

The app says "Not applied" and the `restored` check failed: the router did not answer at its old address 65 s
after a network change.
1. Wait until 2 minutes after the change. rpcd's rollback puts the previous settings back at 60 s.
2. Power-cycle the router. After the rollback, its flash holds the previous settings, so it boots with them. Do
   not power-cycle during the first 60 s: then the flash still holds the change.
3. If it still does not answer, use OpenWrt's failsafe mode: power it on, press a button when the LED blinks fast,
   and reach it at 192.168.1.1 with a cable. Run `firstboot` only if you mean to reset every setting.
```
