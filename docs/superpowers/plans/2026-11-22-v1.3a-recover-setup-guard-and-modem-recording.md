# v1.3a: Recover Waits Out an LTE Setup, and the Modem Recording, Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for
> tracking.

**Goal:** LTE Recover never cuts netifd's `qmi.sh` off in the middle of a setup (#93). A read-only AP2 recording
of the modem's SIM, SMS and USSD answers gives 1.3b (SIM PIN, SMS, USSD) facts to be written from.

**Architecture:**
- **Setup guard (#93):** a new read, `SETUP_RUNNING`, counts `qmi.sh` and `uqmi` processes. Before each rung's
  first command, Recover waits, polling every 2 s for up to 20 s, until no setup runs. If a setup still runs at
  the bound, the rung is skipped, and the rung says so. The ladder goes on, and each later rung waits again.
- **Simulator:** `SimModem` models a setup in progress. It shows how long a setup runs, the gap between netifd's
  retries on a failing link, and the wedge an `ifdown` causes in the middle of a setup (`setup_cut`, cleared only
  by a router reboot).
- **Recording:** a hardware test runs only reads on AP2. These are `uqmi --help`, the SIM's PIN state, the SMS
  list and at most three messages, and AT `?` queries through the app's own AT path. The results are sanitized
  into `tests/fixtures/ap2/modem_sim.json`. 1.3b's decisions are written from that file.
- **One AP2 window**, about 45 minutes. Part 1 is the read-only recording. Part 2 proves the guard on a link made
  to fail on purpose with a wrong APN, then puts the APN back.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13, FastAPI, pydantic, asyncio, pytest; React + TypeScript, Vitest.

**Spec:**
- Issue #93, "LTE Recover: ifdown lte can cut qmi.sh off mid-setup and wedge the modem's QMI channel": its
  evidence, its proposed design and its six open questions. This plan answers them in C2-C7.
- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-lte-recover-design.md`: 4.2 is the ladder, 4.6 the simulator, 4.8 the AP2
  proof, and 4.9 the stock "restore modem defaults".
- `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-17-1.x-inventory-and-split.md`, the row for SIM PIN, SMS and USSD:
  - "Nothing about the BM806C's PIN, SMS or USSD answers is recorded."
  - "AP2's modem and SIM: a read-only recording first. PIN writes can lock the SIM."
  - "`microcom -t 2000` likely cuts a late `+CUSD`."
- `.claude/notes/lte-modem-recovery.md` lists the commands never to send: QMI `reset` mode, `AT&F`, `AT&W`,
  `AT+CFUN=`, and USB composition changes.

**Base:** branch `feat/v1.3a-recover-guard` from master 040c38c (1.3.0-dev), worktree `.claude/worktrees/v13a`,
local.

## Review Focus

1. **A setup that starts during the wait.** The read was 0 at one poll and 1 at the next. Only the poll that
   decides counts: the rung runs right after the first 0, and a setup that starts after that is the residual
   risk C2 accepts. Test in Task 3: a setup that restarts after one idle read still runs the rung once, at the idle
   read.
2. **Stop or a harness run during the wait.** The wait calls `_check` on every poll. Stop ends the job with
   nothing to put back, because no `ifdown` was sent yet. Test in Task 3.
3. **The read fails** (the transport drops, or `ps` errors). That counts as "a setup runs", because the rung must
   not cut what it cannot see. A dropped router then fails the job, as today. Test in Task 2.
4. **A wedge already present at start** (`setup_cut` or `sync_raced` from before). The guard does not hide it:
   every rung runs or skips, and the job ends "not recovered" with the reboot advice. Test in Task 3.
5. **The recording meets a message with a phone number in its text, or a UCS-2 body.** Sanitizing removes both.
   The fixture check (`test_fixtures_sanitized`) and a recorder test with such a message both pass. Test in
   Task 5.
